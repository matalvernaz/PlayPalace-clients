\*\*Mancala\*\*

Mancala is a two-player game of sowing and capturing stones, played here in its Kalah form. Each player has a row of 6 pits and a store at the end of the row. The goal is to finish with more stones in your store than your opponent has in theirs.

\*\*Setup\*\*

Mancala is for exactly 2 players. Every pit starts with the same number of stones, 4 unless the host changes it, and both stores start empty.

Your pits are numbered 1 to 6 from your own side, and your store comes right after your pit 6. Your opponent's pits are numbered the same way from their side. Players take turns in seating order, so the first player seated moves first.

\*\*Your Turn\*\*

Choose one of your own pits that has stones in it. All of its stones are picked up and sown one at a time into the pits that follow: on toward your pit 6, into your store, then into your opponent's pits from their pit 1 to their pit 6, skipping their store, and round again to your own pit 1.

Where the last stone lands decides what happens next:

\* \*\*In your store:\*\* You take another turn straight away.

\* \*\*In an empty pit on your side:\*\* If your opponent's pit directly opposite has any stones, you capture them. They go into your store along with your last stone. If the opposite pit is empty, nothing is captured.

\* \*\*Anywhere else:\*\* Your turn ends.

Your pit 1 faces your opponent's pit 6, your pit 2 faces their pit 5, and so on. A pit's number and the number of the pit facing it always add up to 7.

At the start of every turn, including extra turns, each player hears the board from their own side: your store, your opponent's store, your pits 1 to 6, then your opponent's pits from their pit 1 to their pit 6.

\*\*End of the Game\*\*

The game ends as soon as all six pits on either side are empty. Each player then moves the stones still in their own pits into their own store. The player with more stones in their store wins. If both stores hold the same number, the game is a draw.

\*\*Customizable Options\*\*

\* \*\*Stones per pit:\*\* How many stones each pit holds at the start (default 4, range 1 to 8).

\*\*Keyboard Shortcuts\*\*

\* \*\*1-6:\*\* Sow from that pit on your turn. Your turn menu also lists each pit with its number of stones.

\* \*\*E:\*\* Hear the board: both stores and every pit.

\* \*\*T:\*\* Check whose turn it is.

\*\*Strategy\*\*

\* A pit holding exactly 7 minus its own number of stones ends in your store and earns another turn: 1 stone in pit 6, 2 in pit 5, and so on up to 6 in pit 1.

\* If several pits can reach your store, sow from the one nearest the store first. Sowing a farther pit first drops stones into the nearer ones and spoils their count.

\* An empty pit on your side facing a full enemy pit is a capture waiting to happen. Watch for the same danger to your own full pits.
