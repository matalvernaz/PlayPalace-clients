\*\*Risk\*\*

Risk is a game of world conquest for 2 to 6 players. The board is a map of 42 territories grouped into 6 continents. On your turn you gather new armies, attack neighboring territories with dice, and move troops to strengthen your lines. Wipe out every opponent to win.

\*\*Setup\*\*

The territories are dealt out at random, as evenly as possible, and each starts with 1 troop. The game then scatters extra armies at random across each player's territories, so nobody places armies by hand before the first turn.

With the Starting armies option on 0, which is the default, each player ends up with 40 armies in a two-player game, 35 with three players, 30 with four, 25 with five and 20 with six, counting the troop on each territory. A player who was dealt one more territory than others has one army more. With any other value, every player gets that many armies on top of the troop on each territory.

Players take turns in seating order, starting with the first player seated.

\*\*Your Turn\*\*

Each turn has three phases in order: reinforce, attack and fortify. Your turn menu shows the territories you can choose in the current phase, with their troop counts.

\*\*1\. Reinforce\*\*

You receive new armies: the number of territories you hold divided by 3, rounded down, with a minimum of 3. Add the bonus for each continent you hold completely, and any bonus from trading cards. Place them one at a time by choosing your territories; each choice adds one troop there. When all are placed, the attack phase begins.

\*\*2\. Attack\*\*

First choose a territory to attack from. It must have at least 2 troops and border at least one enemy territory. The menu then lists the enemy territories next to it, with their troops and owners. Choose one to attack, or choose \*\*Cancel attack\*\* to pick a different starting territory.

Each attack is a single roll of the dice:

\* The attacker rolls one die for each troop beyond the first, up to 3 dice. A territory with 4 or more troops rolls 3.

\* The defender rolls 2 dice if the territory has 2 or more troops, otherwise 1.

\* The highest dice are compared in pairs, then the next highest. In each pair the higher die wins, and the defender wins ties. Each lost pair costs that side one troop.

If the defending territory loses its last troop, you conquer it. As many troops as you rolled dice move in automatically, and at least one always stays behind.

After every roll you choose again where to attack from, so to keep pressing the same attack, choose the same two territories again. You may attack as often as you like. Choose \*\*End attack phase\*\* when you are done.

\*\*3\. Fortify\*\*

You may make one move. Choose a territory with at least 2 troops, then a neighboring territory you own. The game moves half of the troops that can leave, rounded down, but at least one: from a territory with 7 troops, 3 move. Your turn then ends. Choose \*\*Skip fortify\*\* to end your turn without moving, or \*\*Cancel fortify\*\* to pick a different territory. A territory with no neighbor of yours has nowhere to send troops.

\*\*Cards\*\*

If you conquered at least one territory during your turn, you get one card when your attack phase ends. Each card shows infantry, cavalry or artillery, and two of the 44 cards are wild. Traded cards are not shuffled back in, so in a long game the cards can run out.

A set is three cards of the same kind, one of each kind, or a wild card with any two others. Cards are traded automatically. Whenever you start your turn holding 5 or more cards, sets are traded one at a time until you hold fewer than 5. You can't trade by hand, and the game doesn't read your cards out, but it announces every trade.

Trades are worth more as the game goes on, counting every trade by any player: 4 armies for the first, then 6, 8, 10, 12, 15, 20, 25, 30, 35, 40, 45, 50, 55 and 60, and 60 for every trade after that.

When you take a player's last territory, they are eliminated and their cards become yours.

\*\*Winning\*\*

The last player holding territories conquers the world and wins.

\*\*The Map\*\*

Each continent's bonus is paid every turn you hold all of it. The territory lists in your menu follow this order.

\* \*\*North America, 9 territories, bonus 5:\*\* Alaska, Northwest Territory, Greenland, Alberta, Ontario, Quebec, Western US, Eastern US and Central America.

\* \*\*South America, 4 territories, bonus 2:\*\* Venezuela, Peru, Brazil and Argentina.

\* \*\*Europe, 7 territories, bonus 5:\*\* Iceland, Scandinavia, Great Britain, Northern Europe, Western Europe, Southern Europe and Ukraine.

\* \*\*Africa, 6 territories, bonus 3:\*\* North Africa, Egypt, East Africa, Congo, South Africa and Madagascar.

\* \*\*Asia, 12 territories, bonus 7:\*\* Ural, Siberia, Yakutsk, Kamchatka, Irkutsk, Mongolia, Japan, Afghanistan, China, India, Siam and Middle East.

\* \*\*Australia, 4 territories, bonus 2:\*\* Indonesia, New Guinea, Western Australia and Eastern Australia.

These are the only links between continents:

\* \*\*North America:\*\* Alaska to Kamchatka in Asia, Greenland to Iceland in Europe, and Central America to Venezuela in South America.

\* \*\*South America and Africa:\*\* Brazil to North Africa.

\* \*\*Europe and Africa:\*\* Western Europe to North Africa, and Southern Europe to North Africa and Egypt.

\* \*\*Europe and Asia:\*\* Ukraine to Ural, Afghanistan and Middle East, and Southern Europe to Middle East.

\* \*\*Africa and Asia:\*\* Egypt and East Africa to Middle East.

\* \*\*Asia and Australia:\*\* Siam to Indonesia.

\*\*Customizable Options\*\*

\* \*\*Starting armies:\*\* Extra armies each player gets at the start, scattered over their territories. 0 means the automatic amounts described under Setup (default 0, range 0 to 50).

\*\*Keyboard Shortcuts\*\*

\* \*\*E:\*\* Check your status: how many territories and troops you have, and how many territories of each continent you hold, with the bonus you are earning there.

\* \*\*T:\*\* Check whose turn it is.

\* \*\*S:\*\* Hear how many territories each player holds. Shift+S shows the same in a box.

\*\*Strategy\*\*

\* Small continents are the easiest to hold. Australia can only be entered through Indonesia, and South America only through Venezuela and Brazil.

\* Attack from territories with 4 or more troops so you roll 3 dice. The defender wins ties, so attacking with fewer dice is costly.

\* Conquer at least one territory every turn to keep earning cards.
