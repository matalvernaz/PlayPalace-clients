\*\*The Game of Life\*\*

The Game of Life is a spin-and-move board game for 1 to 6 players. You spin a wheel numbered 1 to 10 and travel along a track of life events: choose college or a career, collect pay days, perhaps marry and raise children, buy insurance and stock, and finally retire. When everyone has retired, the richest player wins.

\*\*Starting Out\*\*

Everyone starts at space 0 with $10,000, unless the host changes the starting cash. Players take turns in seating order, starting with the first player seated. Each turn begins with an announcement of whose turn it is and their space, such as "Space 12 of 79". The last number is the finish.

Your first turn is spent choosing a path rather than spinning:

\* \*\*Go to college:\*\* You take out a $100,000 college loan and your turn ends. You have no job and no salary until you pass or land on the College Graduation space, about a tenth of the way along. You then pick your career from 3 cards drawn from all nine careers. On the move that gets you there, the space you land on has no effect, and any Pay Day you pass on that move pays nothing.

\* \*\*Start a career:\*\* You pick your career straight away from 2 cards drawn from the four careers that need no degree.

If the host turns the College path option off, there is no choice: on your first turn you pick a career from 2 cards drawn from the four careers that need no degree.

\*\*Careers\*\*

\* \*\*Needing a degree:\*\* Doctor $100,000, Lawyer $90,000, Computer Consultant $80,000, Accountant $70,000 and Teacher $40,000.

\* \*\*Needing no degree:\*\* Athlete $80,000, Entertainer $60,000, Police Officer $50,000 and Salesperson $40,000.

The amount is your salary, paid at every Pay Day.

\*\*Spinning and Moving\*\*

On your turn, spin with \*\*R\*\* or \*\*Space\*\*. You move forward the number spun, but never past the finish. Every time anyone spins to move, each player who owns that stock number collects $10,000.

Most spaces only take effect if you land on them. Two kinds also work when you pass them: Pay Day, and College Graduation for college students.

Money can drop below zero. Nobody is knocked out.

\*\*The Spaces\*\*

\* \*\*Pay Day:\*\* Collect your salary, plus $5,000 for each child when Marriage and children is on. There are four on the board.

\* \*\*Get Married:\*\* There is only one. If you land on it while single, you marry.

\* \*\*Have a Baby:\*\* There are three. If you are married, you have a baby boy or girl, or now and then twins. If you are single, nothing happens.

\* \*\*Insurance:\*\* There are two. You may buy one policy or skip: fire insurance for $20,000, auto insurance for $10,000, life insurance for $15,000, or a full policy for $35,000 that covers all three. You can't buy the same policy twice, and once you hold the full policy you can't buy the others.

\* \*\*Stock:\*\* There are two. If you own no stock and have at least $50,000, you may buy a stock number from 1 to 10 for $50,000. Several players can own the same number.

\* \*\*Risk It:\*\* There are two. If you have at least $25,000, you may stake $25,000 and spin: 7 or higher wins $100,000 and returns your stake, and anything lower loses the stake. Risk It spaces only work when the stock market is on.

\* \*\*Career Change:\*\* There is one. You draw 2 new career cards, from all nine careers if you went to college or from the four no-degree careers otherwise, and must take one of them, even if it pays less.

\* \*\*Event:\*\* Something happens to you, as listed below.

\* \*\*Retirement:\*\* Near the end. Land on it and you retire there; if you pass it, you retire when you reach the finish.

On every board length, the special spaces come in the same order: College Graduation, Get Married, Insurance, Pay Day, Have a Baby, Stock, Risk It, Career Change, Pay Day, Have a Baby, Insurance, Stock, Pay Day, Have a Baby, Risk It, Pay Day, Retirement, and the finish. All other spaces are plain or Event spaces.

\*\*Events\*\*

\* \*\*Windfalls:\*\* Lottery $100,000, best-seller $80,000, inheritance $75,000, tax refund $25,000, promotion $20,000, generous uncle $15,000, garage sale $10,000, money found in a coat $5,000.

\* \*\*Expenses:\*\* Boat $30,000, vacation $20,000, casino loss $20,000, taxes $15,000, broken leg $10,000, new pet $5,000.

\* \*\*Disasters:\*\* Flat tire $5,000 and car accident $15,000, which auto insurance covers; house fire $50,000 and tornado $40,000, which fire insurance covers; hospital stay $25,000, which life insurance covers. A full policy covers them all. Without the right cover, you pay.

\* \*\*Awards:\*\* The Nobel Prize, a design award, helping an elderly neighbor and saving an ecosystem each earn a Life tile. With Life tiles off, they pay $30,000 instead.

\* \*\*Lawsuit:\*\* Only when the stock market is on. Every player still playing spins, and the highest spinner collects $50,000 from the lowest.

\*\*Life Tiles\*\*

Life tiles are worth $20,000 to $200,000 each and count toward your final total. In Hidden mode their values stay secret until the end. In Revealed mode, Check status shows the total value of your tiles.

\*\*Money Matters at Any Time\*\*

Two choices appear in your menu whenever they apply, even on other players' turns:

\* \*\*Sell your stock:\*\* Sells your stock number back for the full $50,000.

\* \*\*Pay off college loan:\*\* Pays the whole balance, or all of your cash if that is less. Until the loan is paid off, every Pay Day also charges $5,000 interest, and whatever you still owe at the end comes off your final cash. It is available once you have at least $10,000, or the whole balance if that is smaller.

\*\*Retirement\*\*

When you retire you choose where to live, then take no more turns:

\* \*\*Countryside Acres:\*\* Collect a $1,000,000 bonus at the end of the game.

\* \*\*Millionaire Estates:\*\* When everyone has retired, every player who chose Estates spins. The highest spinner doubles their cash, and each other Estates player loses $1,000,000. Players who tie for the highest spin all double. If you are the only one at Estates, your spin is the highest.

With the Safe retirement style you still choose, but everyone collects the $1,000,000 bonus and nobody spins. With None, you retire as soon as you land on Retirement or reach the finish, with no bonus.

\*\*End of the Game\*\*

Once every player has retired, the Estates spin-off and the Acres bonuses are settled. Then every Life tile is revealed, and each player's cash, tiles and total are read out. Any college loan you still owe is paid off from your cash first. Your total is then your cash plus your Life tiles. Stock you still own is not counted. The highest total wins. If players tie, they are all announced as winners.

\*\*Customizable Options\*\*

\* \*\*Track length:\*\* Short has 50 spaces, Standard 80 and Long 125, counting the start (default Standard).

\* \*\*College path:\*\* Whether players choose between college and a career at the start (default on).

\* \*\*Marriage and children:\*\* When off, Get Married and Have a Baby spaces do nothing, and Pay Day pays no child bonus (default on).

\* \*\*Life tiles:\*\* Hidden, Revealed or Off (default Hidden).

\* \*\*Insurance:\*\* Full, Simplified or Off (default Full). Full and Simplified both offer all four policies at an Insurance space. With Off, Insurance spaces do nothing and disasters always cost money.

\* \*\*Stock market:\*\* Stock purchases, Risk It spaces and lawsuits (default on). When off, none of them happen.

\* \*\*Retirement style:\*\* Classic, Safe or None, as described under Retirement (default Classic).

\* \*\*Event layout:\*\* With Fixed, the same event sits on the same Event space in every game with the same settings; on a Short board, that means the Event spaces hold only windfalls and expenses. With Random, events are shuffled onto the Event spaces each game (default Fixed).

\* \*\*Starting cash:\*\* Money each player starts with (default $10,000, range 0 to $100,000).

\*\*Keyboard Shortcuts\*\*

\* \*\*R\*\* or \*\*Space:\*\* Spin.

\* \*\*S:\*\* Check your status: your space, career and salary, cash and Life tiles, plus your family, insurance, college loan and stock when those apply.

\* \*\*Shift+S:\*\* Detailed status: every player's space, career, cash, number of Life tiles and family.

\* \*\*T:\*\* Check whose turn it is.

Every other choice, such as picking a career, buying insurance or choosing where to retire, appears in your turn menu when it comes up.

\*\*Strategy\*\*

\* Risk It pays on average. You win 4 spins in 10, and a win gains $100,000 against a $25,000 loss.

\* A stock earns $10,000 each time its number is spun, and you can sell it back for the full $50,000 at any time. A stock you still own at the end isn't counted in your total, so sell it before the last player retires.

\* Insurance is worth buying when the disasters it covers may still be ahead of you: fire insurance costs $20,000 against a $50,000 fire.

\* Millionaire Estates only beats Countryside Acres if you win the spin-off with more than $1,000,000 in cash, and losing it costs $1,000,000.
