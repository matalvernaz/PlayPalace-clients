\*\*Last Card\*\*

Last Card is a shedding card game in the style of UNO for 2 to 10 players. Each round, players race to empty their hands by matching the top card of the discard pile by color, or by number or symbol. Points are scored at the end of each round, and the game ends when someone reaches the winning score.

\*\*The Deck\*\*

Last Card uses 108 cards in four colors: red, blue, green and yellow. Each color has one 0, two of each number from 1 to 9, and two each of Skip, Reverse and Draw Two. There are also 4 Wild cards and 4 Wild Draw Four cards.

\*\*Starting a Round\*\*

Everyone is dealt 7 cards unless the host changes the hand size. The first card turned up to start the discard pile is always a number card. The dealer moves one seat along each round, and the player after the dealer goes first.

\*\*Your Turn\*\*

Play one card that matches the top card by color, or by number or symbol. On a Red 5, you may play any red card or any 5. Wild and Wild Draw Four can be played on anything. After playing one, choose the next color: 1 for red, 2 for blue, 3 for green or 4 for yellow. After a Wild, the next card must match the chosen color, unless it is another Wild.

Your hand is listed in your turn menu, sorted by color unless you change the sort. Choose a card that fits to play it.

If none of your cards can be played, draw one with \*\*Space\*\*. If the drawn card can be played, you may play it. Normally you can only draw when nothing in your hand can be played, and only once a turn. When the deck runs out, the discard pile, apart from its top card, is shuffled to make a new deck.

\*\*Pass\*\* (P) ends your turn. It becomes available after you draw, or straight away whenever you can't draw, for example because a card in your hand can already be played. With Force play on, you can't pass while you hold a card that can be played.

A round ends as soon as a player plays their last card.

\*\*Action Cards\*\*

\* \*\*Skip:\*\* The next player loses their turn.

\* \*\*Reverse:\*\* The direction of play reverses. With 2 players, it acts as a Skip unless the host changes that.

\* \*\*Draw Two:\*\* The next player draws 2 cards and loses their turn.

\* \*\*Wild:\*\* You choose the color to follow.

\* \*\*Wild Draw Four:\*\* You choose the color, and the next player draws 4 cards and loses their turn.

\*\*Challenging a Wild Draw Four\*\*

A Wild Draw Four is only fair if the player had no other card of the color in play. With challenges on, which is the default, the next player gets a few seconds to respond: press \*\*C\*\* to challenge or \*\*A\*\* to accept.

\* If the player who played it did hold a card of the color in play, the challenge succeeds: they draw the 4 cards instead, and you take your turn as normal.

\* If they didn't, the challenge fails: you draw 6 cards and lose your turn.

\* Accepting, or letting the time run out, means you draw 4 cards and lose your turn.

\*\*Calling Last Card\*\*

With the Last card callout rule on, which is the default, a few seconds open up whenever a play leaves a player with one card. That player should press \*\*B\*\*, the Buzzer, to call Last Card; in the web client and the iOS app, Buzzer appears in the turn menu instead. If anyone else presses B first, they catch that player, who draws 2 penalty cards. If nobody presses in time, nothing happens.

\*\*Scoring\*\*

Cards left in hand are worth points:

\* \*\*Number cards:\*\* Their face value, so a 0 is worth nothing.

\* \*\*Skip, Reverse and Draw Two:\*\* 20 points each.

\* \*\*Wild and Wild Draw Four:\*\* 50 points each.

In \*\*Classic\*\* scoring, the default, the player who empties their hand wins the round and scores the points left in everyone else's hands. The first player to reach the winning score, 500 by default, wins the game.

In \*\*Negative\*\* scoring, every player adds the points left in their own hand at the end of each round. When a round ends with anyone at or past the winning score, the game ends and the player with the lowest score wins.

If every player passes in a row while no cards are left to draw, the round ends with no winner. In Classic scoring nobody scores; in Negative scoring everyone adds their hand points as usual.

\*\*House Rules\*\*

These are all off unless the host turns them on.

\* \*\*Stacking:\*\* A player facing a draw penalty may add to it with a matching card, passing a bigger penalty on to the next player. With \*\*Standard\*\*, a Draw Two may be answered with another Draw Two, and a Wild Draw Four with another Wild Draw Four. With \*\*Progressive\*\*, Draw Twos and Wild Draw Fours can be stacked on each other in any mix. While a stack is building, only cards that add to it can be played. A player who can't add to it draws the whole stack and loses their turn. A Wild Draw Four only joins a stack when challenges are off; with challenges on, it goes to the challenge instead.

\* \*\*Skip all, play again:\*\* A Skip skips everyone else, so you take another turn straight away.

\* \*\*Rotate all hands:\*\* Playing a 0 passes every player's hand to the next player in the direction of play.

\* \*\*Swap hand with opponent:\*\* Playing a 7 lets you choose another player to swap hands with. With 2 players, the swap is automatic.

\* \*\*Jump-in:\*\* After most cards are played, the other players get a few seconds to play an identical card, the same color and the same number or symbol, by pressing \*\*J\*\* (in the web client and the iOS app, Jump in appears in the turn menu while the window is open). Play then carries on from the player who jumped in. Only one jump-in can happen before the next player's turn.

\* \*\*Draw until playable:\*\* A card you draw that can't be played lets you draw again, and you keep drawing until you get one that can, or reach the draw limit. You may draw even while you hold a card that can be played, but you can't pass in between draws.

\* \*\*Force play:\*\* You can't draw or pass while you hold a card that can be played, so a playable card you draw must be played.

\* \*\*Multiple card play:\*\* Choosing a card in your hand selects it instead of playing it, and selected cards are read with "(selected)"; choose a card again to unselect it. Then press \*\*X\*\*, or choose the Play item after your hand, to play the selected cards together. They must all share the same number or symbol, at least one must fit the top card, and Wild cards go one at a time.

\*\*Customizable Options\*\*

\* \*\*Winning score:\*\* Score that ends the game (default 500, range 50 to 10,000).

\* \*\*Hand size:\*\* Cards dealt to each player each round (default 7, range 3 to 15). The deck must hold enough cards for everyone.

\* \*\*Turn timer:\*\* 5, 10, 15, 20, 30, 45, 60 or 90 seconds, or Unlimited (default Unlimited). When time runs out, the game moves for you the way a bot would: it plays from your hand if something fits; otherwise it draws a card, which starts the timer again, or passes. If you were choosing a color or a player to swap with, it chooses for you.

\* \*\*Draw until playable:\*\* As described above (default off).

\* \*\*Draw limit:\*\* The most cards you can draw in one turn with Draw until playable; 0 means no limit (default 0, range 0 to 20).

\* \*\*Stacking:\*\* Off, Standard or Progressive (default Off).

\* \*\*Skip card:\*\* Skip next player, or Skip all, play again (default Skip next player).

\* \*\*Reverse in 2-player:\*\* Acts as skip, or Reverse direction (default Acts as skip).

\* \*\*Zero card rule:\*\* None, or Rotate all hands (default None).

\* \*\*Seven card rule:\*\* None, or Swap hand with opponent (default None).

\* \*\*Jump-in:\*\* Default off.

\* \*\*Force play:\*\* Default off.

\* \*\*Last card callout:\*\* Whether players must call Last Card (default on).

\* \*\*Challenge Wild Draw Four:\*\* Default on.

\* \*\*Multiple card play:\*\* Default off.

\* \*\*Scoring mode:\*\* Classic (winner gains) or Negative (losers gain) (default Classic).

\* \*\*Buzzer:\*\* Whether the Buzzer can be used at all. When it is off, nobody can call Last Card or catch anyone (default on).

\* \*\*Reaction timer:\*\* Seconds allowed for calling Last Card, challenges and jump-ins (default 4, range 2 to 7).

\* \*\*Max hand size:\*\* The most cards anyone can hold; drawing, including penalty cards, stops there. 0 means no limit (default 0, range 0 to 50). The hand size can't be larger than this.

\*\*Keyboard Shortcuts\*\*

\* \*\*Space:\*\* Draw a card.

\* \*\*P:\*\* Pass.

\* \*\*1-4:\*\* Choose red, blue, green or yellow after a Wild.

\* \*\*B:\*\* Buzzer: call Last Card, or catch a player who hasn't.

\* \*\*C:\*\* Challenge a Wild Draw Four.

\* \*\*A:\*\* Accept the draw instead of challenging.

\* \*\*J:\*\* Jump in.

\* \*\*X:\*\* Play the selected cards, with Multiple card play on.

\* \*\*W:\*\* Read your hand.

\* \*\*R:\*\* Read the top card, with the chosen color after a Wild.

\* \*\*E:\*\* Read how many cards each player holds and how many are left in the deck.

\* \*\*D:\*\* Check the draw penalty that is building up.

\* \*\*Shift+T:\*\* Check the turn timer.

\* \*\*O:\*\* Change how your hand is sorted: by color, by rank, or unsorted.

\* \*\*S:\*\* Check scores.

\* \*\*Shift+S:\*\* Open detailed scores.

\* \*\*T:\*\* Check whose turn it is.

\*\*Strategy\*\*

\* In Classic scoring, each Wild left in your hand gives 50 points to whoever goes out. Hold one for when you are stuck, but don't sit on several.

\* Watch the card counts. When a player gets down to a card or two, use your action cards to slow them down.

\* Playing a Wild Draw Four while you hold a card of the color in play invites a successful challenge.
