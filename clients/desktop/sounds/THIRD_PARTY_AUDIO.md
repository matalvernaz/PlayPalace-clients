# Third-Party Audio

## Sonniss GDC 2026 Game Audio Bundle

The following files are modified extracts of recordings from the Sonniss GDC
2026 Game Audio Bundle:

- `game_explodingkittens/*.ogg`
- `game_citadels/*.ogg`
- `game_deadmansdeck/*.ogg`
- `game_deadmanspoker/*.ogg`
- `battle/death1.ogg` through `battle/death4.ogg`
- `battle/fall1.ogg` through `battle/fall3.ogg`

They were converted to stereo OGG Vorbis, normalized, and in some cases
trimmed, segmented, or silence-padded to match server sequence timing.

That license permits royalty-free use and modification in games, including

Source bundle: [Sonniss GDC 2026 Game Audio Bundle](https://gdc.sonniss.com/)
