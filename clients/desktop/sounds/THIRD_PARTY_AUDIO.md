# Third-Party Audio

## Sonniss GDC 2026 Game Audio Bundle

The following files are modified extracts of recordings from the Sonniss GDC
2026 Game Audio Bundle:

- `game_explodingkittens/*.ogg`
- `game_citadels/*.ogg`
- `game_deadmansdeck/*.ogg`
- `game_deadmanspoker/*.ogg`
- `battle/death1.ogg` through `battle/death4.ogg`
- `battle/fall1.ogg` through `battle/fall3.ogg`

They were converted to stereo OGG Vorbis, normalized, and in some cases
trimmed, segmented, or silence-padded to match server sequence timing.

The bundle is licensed under the
[Sonniss GDC Game Audio Bundle License](https://sonniss.com/gdc-bundle-license/).
That license permits royalty-free use and modification in games, including
commercial games, without attribution. It does not permit redistributing the
source recordings as a standalone sound library. This notice records
provenance; it does not replace or modify the Sonniss license.

Source bundle: [Sonniss GDC 2026 Game Audio Bundle](https://gdc.sonniss.com/)

## Sonniss GDC Game Audio Bundles, 2015 to 2026 (November 2026 batch)

The following files are modified extracts of recordings from the Sonniss GDC
Game Audio Bundles of 2015 through 2026:

- `game_bang/*.ogg`
- `game_bingo/*.ogg`
- `game_deadmansdice/*.ogg`
- `game_monopoly/*.ogg`
- `game_zombiedice/*.ogg`, except `roll_shake1.ogg` to `roll_shake3.ogg`, which
  are re-encoded copies of this pack's own `game_squares/diceshake*.ogg`
- `notify2.ogg` and `notify3.ogg`

They were converted to mono 48 kHz Ogg Vorbis and levelled, trimmed, padded
with silence to match server timing, and in some cases cut into crossfaded
loops. Each bundle came with its own Sonniss license. The 2024 bundle uses the
license linked above. The 2016 to 2021-2023 bundles use an earlier version of it,
which allows royalty-free use in games without attribution, including editing
the sounds and releasing them as part of a production, and forbids selling the
sounds as they are or claiming authorship of them. The 2015 bundle was released
under what it calls a royalty-free single license.
